Default focus duration

Used when you reset your workspace or start a fresh one. It won't change a task or focus session already in progress.

Starting energy

Choose the energy level to use when beginning a fresh workspace.

Starting preference

Choose what tends to make starting feel difficult. You can change this for each plan.

Reduce decorative motion

Use less animation throughout the app. Your device's reduced-motion preference is respected automatically.

Local progress

Your task list, workspace choices, and wins stay in this browser. An unfinished focus session is kept in this tab so you can resume after a reload; it is cleared when completed or reset.
